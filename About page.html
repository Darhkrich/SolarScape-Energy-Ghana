Company Profile & Corporate Information

SolarScape Energy Ghana Ltd., established in 2019, is Ghana’s premier provider of integrated solar energy solutions. We deliver tailored, high-performance solar PV systems for homes, businesses, and industrial operations nationwide. The company is a registered Ghanaian limited liability entity headquartered in Tema, with operations extending across the country.
