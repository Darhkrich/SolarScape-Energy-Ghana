Company Profile & Corprate Information

Company Profile & Corporate Information SolarScape Energy Ghana Ltd., established in 2019, is Ghana’s premier provider of integrated solar energy solutions. We deliver tailored, high-performance solar PV systems for homes, businesses, and industrial operations nationwide. The company is a registered Ghanaian limited liability entity headquartered in Tema, with operations extending across the country. We specialize in end-to-end solar infrastructure development using only Tier 1, Class-A components designed for durability, efficiency, and reliability in West Africa’s demanding climate. Our offerings encompass grid-tied, off-grid, and hybrid solar installations, all engineered for seamless integration with existing power systems and backup generators. SolarScape’s certified engineers and skilled technicians provide turnkey solutions – from initial system design and installation to ongoing performance monitoring and after-sales support – ensuring each project delivers safe, reliable, long-term clean energy performance. Technology & Components: Our preferred stack includes high-efficiency Tier 1 N-Type solar modules (half-cut mono, low degradation), Deye hybrid inverters (sealed against dust and humidity with intelligent DSP control), and Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery storage with smart BMS (RS485/CAN). These choices are specifically suited to Ghana’s heat, dust, and humidity profiles, maximizing uptime and lifecycle performance while meeting international safety and quality standards.

Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance (Installation & Maintenance Certification) Quality is at the core of SolarScape’s operations. We adhere to rigorous installation standards and best practices to guarantee the safety and performance of every solar PV system we deploy. All installations are carried out under the supervision of certified electrical engineers, in compliance with Ghana’s electrical codes and the Energy Commission’s guidelines for solar PV systems. Our technical team includes Certified Electrical Wiring Professionals (CEWPs) accredited by the Energy Commission, ensuring that all wiring and connections meet national standards for workmanship and safety. We also utilize detailed design simulations and thorough site assessments to verify that each system is optimized for peak efficiency and reliability before implementation. SolarScape maintains a comprehensive internal Quality Assurance/Quality Control (QA/QC) program. This includes multi-stage inspection checkpoints during installation and a rigorous testing and commissioning process for every project. Only high-quality, TÜV SÜD Germany certified components and equipment (Tier 1 solar modules, top-tier Deye hybrid inverters, and industrial-grade surge protection devices) are used, minimizing the risk of failures and enhancing system longevity. We also provide formal warranty coverage on equipment and workmanship, underscoring confidence in the quality of our installations Maintenance & After-Sales Support: We offer 24/7 remote monitoring and diagnostics for installed solar plants, enabling proactive fault detection and rapid response. Our service department conducts preventive maintenance schedules (periodic inspections, module cleaning, thermal/IR checks, torque checks, firmware updates, performance re-tuning) and remains on standby for corrective maintenance when needed. This end-to-end support structure ensures clients’ systems continue to operate at optimal efficiency throughout their lifespan.
